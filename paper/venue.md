# Venue strategy

Verified on 2026-10-05. Recommendation: **the next announced SPLASH-E archival call**, as an educational-tool paper. No future deadline is estimated.

## Best fit: SPLASH-E

The [official 2026 call](https://2026.splashcon.org/track/splash-2026-splash-e) includes educational tools, language design, and integrating research into teaching. It accepts archival papers of 3–10 pages excluding references and appendices, in two-column 10-point ACM `acmart` format with `sigplan,review`, under anonymous review. The 2026 archival deadline was **2026-06-24 AoE** and the non-archival deadline was **2026-09-09 AoE**; both have passed.

The [2025 proceedings](https://sigplan.org/OpenTOC/splash-e25.html) include visualization, proof education, and interactive learning tools. The [2026 program](https://2026.splashcon.org/track/splash-2026-splash-e) and Earle and Bell's [curated semantic mutants paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3841644.3842688) show a relevant audience for executable artifacts and specification-aware assessment. Many accepted papers also include classroom or user evidence that MatrixLang does not yet have. Tool scope is a fit, not a prediction of acceptance.

The concrete gap to address in review is educational significance: an instructor should be able to use the exercises and counterexamples without reproducing the authors' entire development process. The artifact supplies commands, expected observations, and a rubric; a classroom pilot would strengthen usability and learning claims but is not manufactured for this paper.
